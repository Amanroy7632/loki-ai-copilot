## Known limitations
- Push-to-talk is disabled until P4-T04 (speech-to-text); pressing the shortcut explains why.
- The shortcut works only while the window is focused (not a global shortcut).
- With no capture driver, cancel and mute complete cleanup immediately. The P4 driver must send a real `CleanupComplete` after releasing the device (ADR-0017).
- The indicator follows the state machine's desired capture, not an OS-reported open device. The P4 driver should also report device state.

## Security implications
- 5 new commands; status arrives over an IPC Channel (no plugin or event permission).
- The microphone is never opened by this ticket.
- Mute is reachable from both the window and the tray.
